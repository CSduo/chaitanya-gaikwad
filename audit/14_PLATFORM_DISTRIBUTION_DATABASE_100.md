## Strategic Evaluation & Scoring Methodology

Every platform is scored out of **100 points** based on five weighted dimensions:
1. **Commercial Buyer Relevance (35 pts)**: Likelihood that senior procurement managers, interior studio owners, developers, or marketing heads frequent the platform to hire partners.
2. **Domain Authority & Entity Trust (25 pts)**: Authority of the root domain (Moz DA / Ahrefs DR) and its weight in Google's Knowledge Graph / entity corroboration.
3. **Portfolio & Technical Proof Support (15 pts)**: Ability to upload Ultra-HD drawing sheets, 3D renders, video reels, and client methodology.
4. **Review & Reputation Integrity (15 pts)**: Presence of independent, authenticated client reviews (e.g., phone interviews, LinkedIn verification).
5. **Cost vs. Return / Terms Integrity (10 pts)**: Availability of a functional free tier, absence of forced reciprocal links, and strict editorial moderation.

### Tier Classification
- **Tier A (Score 80–100)**: Primary commercial engines. High buyer intent, world-class authority, verified reviews, and essential entity status.
- **Tier B (Score 60–79)**: High-value secondary channels. Excellent niche authority, valuable citation links, and targeted creative/industry exposure.
- **Tier C (Score 40–59)**: Experimental or selective platforms. Good for regional or specialized niche presence, but lower direct lead volume.
- **Reject (Score < 40)**: Unmoderated link farms, scraper sites, or platforms with high spam risk that could harm XIYÀTO's luxury brand equity.
