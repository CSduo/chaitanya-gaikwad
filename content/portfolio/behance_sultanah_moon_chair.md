## 1. Project Overview & Commercial Objective

The Sultanah Moon Chair is a sculptural, curved lounge chair designed for private residences, luxury penthouses, and boutique hospitality lounges. Ahead of commercial production and international launch, the brand required a complete visual asset suite to support:

- E-commerce product configurators (8 distinct fabric and metal finish combinations).
- Print lookbooks and architectural specification sheets (high-resolution 4K and 8K crops).
- A 30-second cinematic film reveal for social channels, luxury exhibitions, and retail displays.

### The Commercial Challenge:
Physical prototyping and international studio photography would have required 8 weeks of shipping logistics, £12,000+ in studio hire and crew fees, and the physical manufacture of 8 separate full-scale chairs before testing market demand.

XIYÀTO was commissioned to execute a complete digital twin campaign from initial CAD boundary files, delivering photorealistic imagery indistinguishable from physical photography in 14 days.

## 2. Technical Production Breakdown

### Phase A: 3D Topology & Digital Twin Modeling
- **Input Data**: Raw conceptual sketches and basic 2D orthographic profiles.
- **Geometry Construction**: Sub-division surface modeling in Blender and 3ds Max. Clean quad-based topology ensuring zero pinching along severe compound curvatures.
- **Micro-Beveling & Seam Detailing**: Every seam incorporates procedural stitch geometry and a 0.8mm edge radius to catch specular rim highlights naturally.
- **Ergonomic Cushion Deformation**: Sculpted memory-foam micro-wrinkles and compression folds applied using ZBrush to introduce the organic imperfections characteristic of handcrafted luxury upholstery.

### Phase B: Material Science & Advanced Shading Nodes
- **Micro-Fiber Velvet Upholstery**:
  - Engineered custom dual-lobe BSDF shader network.
  - Implemented micro-facet sheen distribution to accurately recreate retro-reflection and Fresnel falloff when light grazes curved fabric contours.
  - High-frequency 8K procedural normal maps simulating individual woven fiber strands at macro distances.
- **Champagne Anodised Brass Base**:
  - Anisotropic roughness mapping reproducing directional lathe machining marks.
  - Complex IOR curves calibrated to true metal reflectivity values.
- **Hand-Finished Dark Walnut Accents**:
  - Multi-layered diffuse, roughness, and 32-bit displacement maps capturing open-pore wood grain depth.

### Phase C: Studio Lighting & Cinematic Camera Choreography
- **Virtual Studio Setup**: 
  - Dual 200cm octabox key lights with 5500K daylight-balanced emitters.
  - Linear strip rim lights positioned at 135-degree opposing angles to delineate silhouette contours against deep, moody backdrops.
  - Subtle bounce cards for shadow gradient preservation without flattening contrast.
- **Cinematic Lens Simulation**:
  - Emulated 50mm and 85mm anamorphic prime lenses at f/2.8 to achieve optical depth-of-field falloff.
  - Calibrated physical camera exposure, sensor noise floor, and optical vignetting.
- **Motion & Dynamics**:
  - 24fps orbit and macro tracking shots utilizing cubic bezier camera curves to convey weight, craftsmanship, and tactile material depth.
