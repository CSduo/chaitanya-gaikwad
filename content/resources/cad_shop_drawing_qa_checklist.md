# Fit-Out Shop Drawing Quality Assurance & Coordination Checklist (25-Point Standard)

**Publisher**: XIYÀTO CAD & Technical Production Desk (https://xiyato.uk/services/cad/interior-fit-out-shop-drawings)  
**Standard**: BS 1192 / AIA CAD Layer Standards & Commercial Fit-Out Coordination  
**Application**: Interior Architecture, Joinery Fabrication, Luxury Hospitality, Retail Fit-Out  

---

## 1. Drawing Setup & Layer Structure Discipline
- [ ] **1. Unified Layer Hierarchy**: All drawing elements reside on strict named layers (e.g., `A-WALL-FULL`, `A-FLOR-PATT`, `A-JOIN-ELEV`, `M-HVAC-CDFF`). Zero entities drawn on `Layer 0` or `Defpoints` (except non-printing viewports).
- [ ] **2. ByLayer Discipline**: Color, linetype, and lineweight must strictly be set to `ByLayer`. Zero manual object-level overrides.
- [ ] **3. Plot Style Table Compliance**: CTB / STB plot styles mapped to verifiable lineweights (0.09mm hatching, 0.18mm dimensions, 0.25mm secondary lines, 0.50mm cut geometry).
- [ ] **4. Scale & Unit Integrity**: Model space drawn 1:1 in true millimeters. Zero scaling of geometry in model space.
- [ ] **5. External Reference (Xref) Cleanliness**: All architectural backgrounds and MEP overlays attached as `Overlay` rather than `Attach` to prevent circular reference errors.

---

## 2. Setting-Out & Tolerances
- [ ] **6. Structural Datum References**: Every GA plan references verified building gridlines or structural column centers.
- [ ] **7. Plumb & Level Tolerances**: Minimum 15mm–25mm clearance allowed between bespoke joinery subframes and base building blockwork/drywall to accommodate site variances.
- [ ] **8. Movement & Deflection Gaps**: Structural slab deflection joints (15mm–20mm) detailed at top of full-height partitions and ceiling bulkheads.
- [ ] **9. Finish Floor Level (FFL) Consistency**: Floor finishes and threshold transition strips coordinated across wet and dry areas (e.g., stone to engineered timber).
- [ ] **10. Ceiling Plenum Clearances**: Minimum 120mm clearance maintained above reflected ceiling plans for recessed architectural lighting and low-profile ducting.

---

## 3. Joinery, Millwork & Detail Fabrication
- [ ] **11. Substrate & Core Specification**: Moisture-resistant MDF (MR MDF), plywood, or solid timber cores clearly designated with exact thicknesses (e.g. 18mm, 25mm).
- [ ] **12. Finished Face & Edging**: Veneer grain direction (horizontal/vertical) and 2mm solid matching lippings explicitly scheduled.
- [ ] **13. Hardware Mortise Clearances**: Concealed European hinges (e.g. Blum Onyx / Hafele) drawn with exact cup depth (13mm) and edge setback (5mm).
- [ ] **14. Integrated LED Lighting Details**: Continuous aluminum extrusion channels, frosted diffusers, and concealed driver access panels detailed.
- [ ] **15. Cable Management & Service Access**: 60mm grommets, brush plates, and removable back panels detailed for all power and data locations.

---

## 4. Multi-Discipline Clash Coordination
- [ ] **16. Sprinkler Head Alignment**: Ceiling sprinkler heads coordinated to center on architectural ceiling grid panels.
- [ ] **17. AC Diffuser / Linear Slot Integration**: Supply and return air slots integrated into architectural shadow gaps and joinery bulkheads.
- [ ] **18. Structural Beam & Column Encasements**: Framing substrate verified against structural engineer's as-built steel/concrete dimensions.
- [ ] **19. Plumbing Fall Coordination**: Waste pipe gradients (1:40 minimum) coordinated under raised vanity counters and bar counters.
- [ ] **20. Access Hatches**: Clean architectural access panels specified below all concealed MEP valves, FCU filters, and audio-visual racks.

---

## 5. Drawing Output & Issue Verification
- [ ] **21. North Point & Key Plan**: Accurate orientation and highlighted location key present on every sheet.
- [ ] **22. Title Block & Revision Metadata**: Project title, drawing number, scale, date, author initials, and explicit revision letter (e.g. Rev A - Issued for Construction).
- [ ] **23. Revision Clouding**: Delta clouds and revision triangles placed around all modified details since previous issue.
- [ ] **24. Dimension Completeness Check**: Dual-dimensioning strings (overall running string + individual element string) checked for math closure.
- [ ] **25. Dual-Peer QA Review**: Independent senior technician sign-off verified prior to final PDF/DWG package export.
