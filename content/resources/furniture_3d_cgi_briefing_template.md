## 4. Master Camera Shot List (Standard Commercial Asset Suite)

### Still Imagery (3840 × 2160 @ 300 DPI)
- [ ] **Shot 1: The 45-Degree Hero Shot**: Camera at eye-level (sitting height, ~900mm), 85mm focal length, f/4 aperture. Captures full proportion, silhouette, and primary fabric drape.
- [ ] **Shot 2: Front Elevation Studio Profile**: Camera orthographic or 120mm telephoto lens. Clean geometric presentation against seamless gradient cyclorama.
- [ ] **Shot 3: Rear Three-Quarter Silhouette**: Highlights rear framing joinery, spine contour, and upholstery back-panel tension.
- [ ] **Shot 4: Overhead Ergonomic Plan**: 45-degree elevated angle displaying cushion depth and armrest curvature.
- [ ] **Shot 5: Macro Textile Close-Up (Seam Focus)**: 100mm macro lens, f/2.8 shallow depth of field. Focuses on seam stitch precision and micro-fiber light retro-reflection.
- [ ] **Shot 6: Macro Material Detail (Hardware / Joint)**: Captures wood-to-metal joinery tolerance and anisotropic surface reflections.
- [ ] **Shot 7: Contextual Lifestyle Scene**: Furnished boutique residential or hotel penthouse interior with atmospheric morning/afternoon daylight.

### Cinematic Motion Suite (4K 60fps / 24fps Delivery)
- [ ] **Motion 1: 360-Degree Continuous Orbit**: 10-second smooth orbit revealing three-dimensional form.
- [ ] **Motion 2: Slow Raking Macro Tracking**: 5-second camera glide across fabric weave catching rim light highlights.
- [ ] **Motion 3: Exploded Assembly Reveal**: Digital twin breakdown displaying internal kiln-dried hardwood framing, webbing, foam cores, and upholstery layers.
